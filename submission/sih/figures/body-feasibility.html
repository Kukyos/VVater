Measured finding: model error by depth

Argo (the model already used them) Glider (independent)

Below 300 m model and instruments agree (0.35 vs 0.31 °C). In the top 300 m — the warm layer a cyclone feeds on — the independent glider finds 2.89 °C. That is the gap this tool makes visible.
