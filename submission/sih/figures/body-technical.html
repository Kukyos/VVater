From raw files to the scene — and what happens to bad or missing data

Diamonds are decisions the code makes. The red and amber branches are where most tools quietly drop a bad reading or paint over a gap; here both stay on screen, labelled.

What we used, and why

  • CesiumJSa real 3D globe with a built-in volume renderer
  • TypeScript + Vitethe viewer; no heavy UI framework
  • Python: FastAPI + xarrayreads the NetCDF and text files the brief names
  • Raw float32 volumesone full 3D timestep is 0.55 MB, straight to the GPU
  • OGC WMSthe same layers open in QGIS or any national portal
  • Groq LLM + our own toolsthe assistant can only answer through our data API
